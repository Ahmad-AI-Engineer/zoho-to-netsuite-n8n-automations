# Invoice Automation

## Overview

This workflow automates the process of syncing invoice data from Zoho Billing to Oracle NetSuite using n8n.

The goal of this automation is to reduce manual invoice entry, improve finance process accuracy, and keep invoice records aligned between Zoho Billing and NetSuite.

## Business Purpose

Many finance teams use Zoho Billing for customer billing and NetSuite for accounting or ERP operations. Without automation, invoice data may need to be manually copied between both systems.

This workflow helps automate that process by moving invoice-related data from Zoho Billing into NetSuite.
