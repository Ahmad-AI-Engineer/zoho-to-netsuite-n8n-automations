# Credit Memo Automation

## Overview

This workflow automates the process of syncing credit memo data from Zoho Billing to Oracle NetSuite using n8n.

The goal of this automation is to reduce manual credit memo handling, improve finance accuracy, and keep refund or adjustment records aligned between Zoho Billing and NetSuite.

## Business Purpose

Credit memos are commonly used for refunds, adjustments, billing corrections, and customer credits.

When Zoho Billing and NetSuite are used together, credit memo data may need to be manually recreated in NetSuite. This workflow helps automate that process and reduces manual finance work.
